OpenAI and Google Backed One robots.txt Opt-Out. The Draft Says It Stops No Crawl

On Friday, October 30, OpenAI and Google each published support for a single shared token in robots.txt that is meant to express AI training preferences to every provider at once. The draft they are pointing at is an individual Internet-Draft, not a standard. It states that the token “is not a directive for crawling behavior”, it lets private agreements override it, and no clause requires any provider to obey it. Neither company has implemented it.
