Known limits

  • Single primary source, and that source is the subject of the article. No independent forensic examination has been published.
  • No interview with Cloudflare or with the researcher who found the bug.
  • Statements about severity and impact are Cloudflare's assessments and are attributed as such rather than adopted.
  • The coverage measurement rests on a Hacker News query alone; the general web-search instrument returned unusable results during the drafting run, so no claim is made about mainstream coverage.
  • The SGLang issue tracker was not independently retrieved. The article reports issue #30233 as Cloudflare describes it.
