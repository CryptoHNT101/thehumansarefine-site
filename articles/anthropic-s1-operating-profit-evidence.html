Addendum — 2026-10-29, authorised correction A4 before publication

The Commander authorised one correction to the article after the consolidated review (reports/batch003-package-01-review.md), recorded there as observation A4.

What changed. The paragraph decomposing “Total other income (expense), net” now follows the issuer’s own line order in the statement of operations — interest income, then change in fair value, then other income (expense), then the total — instead of presenting the fair-value line first and deducting interest income last. One clarifying clause was added: the statement presents these items as positive magnitudes, each expense figure adds to the reported net loss, the interest income offsets it, and the provision for income taxes adds to it.

What did not change. No figure, period, sign or calculation was altered. The identity the article now prints — 693 plus 72,388 plus 8,757 less 1,111 plus 21 equals 80,748 — is the same arithmetic as before, restated in the order the statement itself uses, and it is exactly equivalent to the decomposition recorded at claim 16 above and at R1:

  • Total other income (expense), net: (80,034)
  • of which interest income 1,111 (income), change in fair value (72,388), other income (expense), net (8,757)
  • 1,111 − 72,388 − 8,757 = −80,034
  • operating loss (693) + total other (80,034) + provision for income taxes (21) = net loss (80,748)

Why it is not a factual change. A4 was a presentation defect only. The components, their signs and their periods were already correct; the article listed them in an order the statement does not use, and described them as “less interest income” after the fact rather than in the statement’s sequence. The correction removes that discrepancy without introducing any new interpretation or altering a verified calculation.
