What this file does not establish

It does not independently verify Anthropic’s 70 per cent and 20 per cent figures. Those are the company’s own measurements of its own migration, published by the company, and the report says so.

It does not establish that any weakened assertion reached a customer, or that none did. The post does not say either way, and the absence claims above record only that.

It is not a legal review. Nothing here is legal advice.
