Known limits

  • Two of the studies cited were verified at abstract level only: the null secondary-school trial and the small primary-school writing study. Both full texts sat behind a paywall and a challenge page and were not read.
  • The OECD’s synthesis was read at the level of its published key messages. The studies underpinning it were not retrieved and are not attributed to The Humans Are Fine.
  • Every study cited measures weeks. None follows a child across a schooling or a worker across a career, so no claim is made about long-term effects.
  • No evidence on reading or early writing in elementary schooling was found that would survive scrutiny, and the article says so rather than generalising.
  • No study comparing handwritten with typed work on comprehension was retrieved. The article therefore makes no claim either way about whether the medium matters.
