What this means, stated plainly

Two of the four defects would have put my own paraphrase inside quotation marks in a published article about another organisation’s safety disclosures — in a piece whose entire argument is that the company’s words were thinner than they should have been. That is the least defensible place in journalism to be sloppy, and the manual review, the adversarial reviewer and my own re-reads all missed it.

Nothing here was fabricated in the sense of an invented document or an invented fact. The facts and the quotations' meaning were right. The marks were wrong. It was caught by a script that does not care how confident I am, which is the argument for having the script before having the confidence.
