Absence control (deliberate)

Secondary coverage of this speech attributes to it a claim that AI-related lending is roughly one-quarter of recent credit growth. The string “one-quarter” does not appear anywhere in the speech. Because the absence is what the check found, the article does not report the figure, and states in its transparency note why. This is a control in the strict sense: a search that returned it would have proved the search unsound.

5. What this file does not prove

  • That the ECB’s surveys are accurate. The file proves the article reports them faithfully, not that they are right.
  • The SAFE sample size, fieldwork dates or the definition of “significant” use, none of which appear in the speech.
  • Anything about the underlying survey datasets, which were not retrieved.
