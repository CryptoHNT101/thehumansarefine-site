Known limits

  • Single publisher: both sources are GOV.UK documents from the Home Office. No second outlet, parliamentary record, or police force statement was retrieved, so nothing here is independently corroborated.
  • The consultation response, any draft bill, and the College of Policing's Deloitte contract were not retrieved; the watchdog's powers are described only as the announcement describes them.
  • The crime-reduction percentages and the algorithm's accuracy claim rest on the Home Office's account; no underlying data, methodology or evaluation report was retrieved.
  • The factsheet is guidance rather than law: what PoliceAI will and will not do reflects current policy and could change, and its section on facial recognition refers to a separate factsheet that was not retrieved.
  • The factsheet's note that Police and Crime Commissioners remain the accountability route 'until the positions are abolished and replaced' is quoted as written; no source retrieved this run describes that change.
