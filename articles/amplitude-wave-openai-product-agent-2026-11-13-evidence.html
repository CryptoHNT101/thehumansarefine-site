Quotations

Four direct quotations are used, all tested character for character against the retrieved page: the 1-2 day estimate; Eric Carlson on non-groundbreaking improvements; Spenser Skates on models finding useful work; and the roughly-80%-OpenAI statement. Attributions (Carlson, Senior Principal AI Engineer; Skates, chief executive) match the page. One 160% figure is attributed to Amplitude's readout rather than to OpenAI.

What this document does not contain

  • No independent audit of the 1-2 day shipping estimate.
  • No sample size for the Opportunity Investigator or Data Science Agent evaluations.
  • No full evaluation harness: criteria lists are published, but prompts, scoring rubrics, weighting and adjudication are not.
  • No names for the alternative models in either comparison.

Known limits

  • Single-origin evidence. The only document is published by OpenAI about a customer, and the customer is quoted inside it. Nothing here has been verified outside the two companies.
  • The 160% lift is one A/B test, on the vendor's own homepage, reported by the vendor.
  • No one at Amplitude, OpenAI or any regulator was interviewed. No independent expert was asked to assess the cost or latency claims.
  • The evaluation figures are reproduced as published. The underlying test sets, prompts and scoring scripts were not retrieved and are not claimed to have been.
