Correction made during verification

CNN's phrase "the second such breach in recent months" has an ambiguous referent: the prior incident is the September 2026 ShinyHunters jobs-website attack, which was not an NCIC intrusion, and CNN separately references an earlier breach of an FBI New York field office system.

The draft originally rendered this as "The breach is the second of its kind in recent months," which could be read as a second NCIC breach. That reading is not supported. The passage was rewritten to state the ambiguity explicitly and to name the earlier FBI New York field office breach. Recorded here as a correction rather than silently edited, because the distinction between "second breach of the FBI" and "second breach of NCIC" is precisely the kind of shorthand this article argues against.
