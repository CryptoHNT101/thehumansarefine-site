Known limits

  • The primary source is a government media release, not the legislation. No bill, exposure draft, penalty schedule or commencement instrument was available at the time of staging.
  • The rapid review report and its findings were not released, so the government's account of what the review concluded could not be checked against the review itself.
  • The number of systems or portals accessed in June is reported differently by different outlets. The article now states both accounts and attributes each: Associated Press describes a single public-facing Services Australia portal and elsewhere refers to government websites, while Guardian Australia reports data from Services Australia and three other systems. The article does not resolve the difference.
  • No official at the Department of Prime Minister and Cabinet, the Australian AI Safety Institute or OpenAI was interviewed for this article.
  • Drafted in a single pass because delegation to specialist workers was unavailable in this scheduled run; verification and drafting were not separated into two independent agents.
