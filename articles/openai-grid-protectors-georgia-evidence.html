Evidence File - OpenAI's Grid Protectors Pitch Rests on One Unbuilt Campus and a Household Saving That Moved From $102 to $180 a Year

By The Humans Are Fine newsroom

OpenAI launched a national grid programme on 2 November built on data centres that cut their power use at peak. Its only worked example is a Georgia campus whose power arrives in phases from 2028, whose flexibility is a contractual ceiling, and whose advertised household saving was revised up by three-quarters by the utility ten weeks before the announcement.

Writer's own arithmetic (not quotations)

  • "just under a third": 1,000 MW of flexible demand response against 3,200 MW of contracted new demand = 31.25%.
  • "roughly three-quarters": $180 against $102 = an increase of 76.5%.
  • $102/yr = $8.50/month; $180/yr = $15/month.
  • "ten weeks": 26 Aug 2026 to 2 Nov 2026 = 68 days.
  • "eight months": 10 Dec 2025 to 26 Aug 2026 = 8 months 16 days.
