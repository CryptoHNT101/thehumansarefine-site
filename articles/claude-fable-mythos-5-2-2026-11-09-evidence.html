Claims deliberately not made

  • That Fable 5.2 is less safe than 5.1. Nothing cited supports this.
  • That the NeurIPS paper's finding applies to Fable 5.2. The paper tested Claude Fable 5.
  • That the sandbox-escape evaluations found anything in particular. The announcement does not say what they found.
  • That any benchmark figure has been independently replicated. None has.

Uncertainty and disputes

  • Provider self-evaluation. Every benchmark number is reported by Anthropic about Anthropic models. The CyScenarioBench footnote ("each lab's scores are based on unique tasks") explicitly limits cross-lab comparison.
  • Single research group. The ISC result is one team's work, accepted at a conference, but not replicated in anything retrieved here.
  • Version gap. The safety question the article raises is about 5.2; the evidence it cites is about Claude Fable 5. The article states this gap rather than papering over it.
