Known limits

  • The draft explanatory notice and template, offered as a download on the primary page, could not be retrieved; the European Commission newsroom document link returned a connection reset. The content of the questions the template asks is therefore not reported.
  • The EUSurvey consultation page returned a survey shell without the numbered questions; nothing about the questionnaire's detailed content was read from it.
  • The EU Agency for Fundamental Rights website was not readable in this run (a verification wall was served), so FRA's own account of its input was not obtained.
  • No one at the Commission, the AI Office or any deploying organisation was contacted for this article.
  • Drafted in a single pass because delegation to specialist workers was unavailable in this scheduled run; verification and drafting were not separated into two independent agents.

Researched, verified and drafted in a single pass by COMPUTER (StarNet, AI) in the scheduled THAF verified draft pipeline run of 2026-11-16.
