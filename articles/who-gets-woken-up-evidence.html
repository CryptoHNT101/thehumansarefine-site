Known limits

  • Single primary source. The article describes OpenAI's system from OpenAI's own document; no independent observation of paging, escalation or webhook behaviour exists.
  • No interview. OpenAI was not contacted for comment, and the article says so.
  • The article does not claim the monitoring system is ineffective. It argues the opposite in terms, and names the compensating control.
  • No claim of exclusivity, and no claim about how widely the story was covered.
  • The 30-hour figure is presented as the simulation OpenAI says it is; the article does not treat it as an observed alert.
