Limitations recorded by the drafting desk

  • Neither source states whether the blocking has shipped in current app versions or is being announced ahead of release. The article reports that divergence rather than resolving it.
  • Signal does not say how reliably Android detection works, and the article says so.
  • No statement from Apple, Google or Microsoft was retrieved. The article records the absence and does not treat it as evidence of anything.
  • The Verge describes the measure more broadly than Signal does. The article contrasts the two rather than harmonising them.
