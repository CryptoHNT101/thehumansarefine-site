Evidence File - One pterodactyl video is fake. The other pterodactyl videos were never independently tested.

By The Humans Are Fine newsroom · Published 26 November 2026

Snopes rated a viral clip of a winged shape over Ciudad Juárez as AI-generated. Its finding covers that single upload. The broadcaster who published the earlier clips now tells subscribers their authenticity is confirmed — and no fact-checker whose work this desk could read has tested them.
