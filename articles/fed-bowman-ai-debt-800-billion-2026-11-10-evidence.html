Known limits

  • The primary source is the published speech text. Any question-and-answer session at the Council on Foreign Relations is not part of that text and was not available to this run.
  • The Bloomberg report of the same speech is paywalled and was not opened; the secondary source used here is MLQ.ai, which cites it.
  • The $800 billion and three-times-debt figures are Bowman's, reproduced from her speech. No underlying data series was retrieved to test them.
  • No official at the Federal Reserve, at any listed AI firm or at the Council on Foreign Relations was interviewed for this article.
