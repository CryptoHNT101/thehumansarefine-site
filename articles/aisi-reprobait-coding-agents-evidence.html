Known limits

  • Single institutional source. No independent replication of ReproBAIT was located.
  • The preprint was not read; all figures come from the Institute's own pages.
  • It is not established whether the developer of CMD 1 was consulted.
  • It is not established whether the published cost figures cover every attempt or only successful ones.
  • The memorisation explanation is the Institute's reading of its own diagnostics, hedged by the Institute.
