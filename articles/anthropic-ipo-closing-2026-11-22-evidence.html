The $4,400 gap is exactly the difference between the stated discount total ($550,000,000) and the discount priced per share ($0.56122 x 980,000,000 = $549,995,600). The two discount figures agree only to five decimal places: $550,000,000 / 980,000,000 = $0.561224489796, printed on the cover as $0.56122, and $83.00 - $0.56122 = $82.43878 exactly. The residue is therefore capable of two readings - the discount total as $549,995,600 rounded to the nearest million, or the per-share discount as the exact quotient carried to five decimals - and the filing does not say which figure it treats as authoritative. The article presents both and adopts neither. The two proceeds totals reproduce from the per-share figure to the dollar ($78,840,438,875.11 and $1,949,565,524.89, whose roundings cancel), which is a weaker claim than exact reproduction and is stated as such.

What this Evidence File does not establish

  • It does not establish that the option was exercised. The only source saying so is secondary.
  • It does not establish that the offering closed, only that delivery was expected on or about 20 November.
  • It does not establish a market capitalisation or a post-offering market value. Claims 33-35 are arithmetic at the offer price, not a statement that the company is worth that amount on any given day.
  • It does not establish the contents of the free-writing prospectuses, which were not individually read. The full-text index covers them and returns no hit for the headline figure.
  • It does not establish how many shares were actually allocated, as distinct from registered and offered.
