3. Context that is NOT from a retrieved source

The description of committed cloud spend — that enterprises pre-commit spending in exchange for discounts, and that unspent commitment is money paid for nothing — is general industry practice, not drawn from any AWS contract or pricing document reviewed for this article. The article presents it as the standard arrangement, not as a specific contractual term. The only sourced elements of that passage are the two vendor lines that name EDP and PPA and the buyer-guide sentence about private offers.

4. Not verified, and not claimed in the article

  • Any price or discount, and whether marketplace pricing matches buying direct. The sources state product parity, not price parity.
  • What “eligible” means for the Healthcare and Financial Services products.
  • The accuracy of the 1.2 billion weekly-user figure.
  • Channel revenue, customer counts, contract terms, support division and data residency.
  • What the retrieval history actually was. The search results page was retrieved, and both listing cards were read from its rendered text by the station browser on 11 November 2026. The product-detail page for ChatGPT Enterprise — the page carrying pricing, data residency and support terms — was not retrieved. Earlier versions of this file said the listing “was not retrieved”, which conflated the two and contradicted the article’s own Sources section. That sentence has been removed.
