Known limits

  • Single-event, single-publisher story: both documents read this run derive from the same government press release. No delivery partner, council officer or participant was contacted, and no outcome data exists yet.
  • The £810,591 headline figure is the newsroom's sum of four published grants. The government describes the total only as 'over £800,000'. The figure is disclosed as arithmetic in the standfirst, the body, the fact-check notes and the Evidence File.
  • The £3,377 figure is illustrative division, not a measured cost. Because the college award carries no headcount and the small-business award counts firms rather than staff, true reach is higher than 240 and the real per-person figure would be lower. The release does not supply what is needed to calculate it, so the article does not assert one.
  • Barnsley Council's page is dated 02 November 2026 but uses 'today (Tuesday 3 November)' in its body text. The discrepancy was observed, not explained; the gov.uk publication date of 3 November 2026 is the date used in the article.
  • The secondary document is Barnsley Council carrying the same release: a different publisher, not a second source. It reproduces the same paragraphs and quotations and omits the four grant figures, so it corroborates the wording of the announcement and not the facts behind it.
