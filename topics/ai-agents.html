AI SAFETY & DISCLOSURE // CODING AGENTS

Anthropic Tests Reveal Weaknesses in AI Coding-Agent Security Monitors

Anthropic's red-team research found substantial bypass success against the blocking monitors used in Claude Code's Auto Mode and in OpenAI's Codex, the Guardian variant tested being an approximation of Codex's production default. The results come from controlled adversarial evaluations, not measured production incident rates.

AI SAFETY & DISCLOSURE // AGENT MONITORING

An Outside Red Team Found Ways Around OpenAI's Own Agent Monitors

METR and Redwood Research stress-tested OpenAI's internal misalignment monitoring. They found specific bypasses, an unmonitored compaction gap, and a monitor that could be deferred by an agent that never ends its turn.
