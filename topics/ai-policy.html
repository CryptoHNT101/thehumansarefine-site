England's Most Senior Civil Judge Says Chatbots Are Driving a Surge in Court Claims. The Government's Own Statistics Point at Parking Tickets.

Days from retirement, Geoffrey Vos says a 10 percent annual rise in county court claims is down to people consulting Gemini, ChatGPT and Claude. The Ministry of Justice's own quarterly bulletin, published in September, points at claims over parking on private land — and shows the category he wants automated getting smaller.
